Shipping & Returns

1. Shipping Timelines

Items are posted directly by the seller. Sellers are asked to dispatch within 3 working days of your order, and UK delivery usually takes a further 2 to 5 working days depending on the service used.

2. Shipping Costs

Each seller sets their own postage price, which is shown on the listing and again at checkout before you pay. If you buy from more than one seller, postage is charged separately for each, as your items will arrive in separate parcels.

3. Order Tracking

You will receive an email confirming your order, and you can check the status of any order from the Orders page in your account. Where a seller uses a tracked service, they will share the tracking details with you.

4. Returns Policy

You can request a return within 14 days of delivery if an item arrives damaged, faulty, or not as described. As most items are secondhand, please read the condition notes and photos carefully before buying. Returns for a change of mind are accepted where the seller's listing says so.

5. How to Start a Return

Go to the Orders page in your account, or use our contact page, and tell us your order number and what is wrong. Photos help. Once the return is approved, send the item back in the condition you received it.

6. Refunds

Refunds are issued to your original payment method once the seller confirms the item has been received. Please allow 5 to 10 working days for the money to appear. If an item was damaged or not as described, your original postage cost is refunded too.

7. Damaged or Lost Items

If your parcel arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours with photos of the item and packaging. If nothing has arrived within 10 working days of dispatch, let us know and we will work with the seller to trace it, replace it, or refund you.
